Pillar 03 • Dubai Direct

UAE 10-Year Golden Visa

HOVER TO EXPAND

The UAE Golden Visa gives investors, entrepreneurs, and skilled professionals the right to live in Dubai for 10 long years without needing a local sponsor. It is one of the most popular long-term residency options in the world because of Dubai's safe environment, top-notch schools, and zero personal income tax.

Our office in Business Bay, Dubai works directly with government authorities and the Dubai Land Department. We handle your property evaluations, medical test bookings, and Emirates ID paperwork quickly so you get your visa approved with zero hassle.
