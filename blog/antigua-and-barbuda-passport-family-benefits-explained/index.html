Antigua & Barbuda Passport Family Benefits Explained

Sharif Group Advisory Desk August 2026 Last Updated: August 2026

When selecting a citizenship by investment program, family inclusivity is often the most important factor for high-net-worth investors. The Antigua and Barbuda program stands out as the most family-friendly option in the global migration market.

The legislation allows a single primary applicant to include up to four generations of family members under one unified application. This enables parents, children, grandparents, and unmarried siblings to secure permanent Commonwealth citizenship together.

Inclusion of Dependent Children Up to Age 30

Under Antiguan citizenship regulations, biological and legally adopted children up to the age of 30 can be included as dependents in the main application file.

Unlike programs that set strict age limits at 18 or 21, Antigua allows adult children who are currently enrolled in higher education or financially dependent on the main applicant to qualify.

Children with physical or mental disabilities of any age are also fully eligible for inclusion, guaranteeing them lifelong care, international security, and unrestricted global mobility.

Sponsoring Parents and Grandparents Over 55

The program allows primary applicants to include their parents and grandparents, as well as the parents and grandparents of their spouse.

The qualifying age threshold is set at 55 years or older, provided they are financially supported by the principal applicant. This allows elderly family members to enjoy the safety, freedom, and healthcare benefits of a second citizenship without complex independent applications.

All included parents receive full, independent Certificates of Naturalization and lifetime Commonwealth passports.

Inclusion of Unmarried Dependent Siblings

A distinctive feature of the Antigua and Barbuda program is the ability to include unmarried biological or legally adopted siblings of either the main applicant or the spouse.

Many other citizenship programs strictly prohibit siblings from being included under a single family file. Antigua recognizes extended family dynamics, allowing you to protect your brothers and sisters under one investment.

Siblings of any age can be added, provided they are unmarried and financially dependent on the primary investor.

The University of the West Indies (UWI) Educational Advantage

For large families with six or more members, the University of the West Indies (UWI) Fund route provides unmatched economic and educational value.

Under this pathway, a flat contribution of $300,000 USD covers a family of six, with each additional dependent requiring a modest fee. This makes it substantially cheaper per person than traditional donation options.

In addition, the route includes a full, tuition-free one-year scholarship for one dependent family member at the accredited University of the West Indies campus in Antigua, opening up world-class higher education opportunities.

Generational Inheritance and Post-Citizenship Additions

Antiguan citizenship is permanent and automatically inheritable. Newborn children born after citizenship is granted are registered as Antiguan citizens by descent.

If the main applicant marries after receiving their passport, the new spouse and future children can be added to the citizenship registry through a streamlined post-approval application.
