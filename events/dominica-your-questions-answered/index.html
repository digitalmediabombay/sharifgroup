Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install an app?

No. The link opens in your phone or computer browser. Chrome works best.

Where is the joining link?

On this page — it is open to everyone. It is posted shortly before the session and the room opens 10 minutes before the start. If you register, we also send it to you on WhatsApp.

Will others see my name or face?

No. You can use any name, your camera and microphone are off, and the recording only shows the speakers and slides.

What if my internet is weak?

Choose “audio only”. The voice is the heart of the session and the slides are simple.

Is it free?

Yes, attending is completely free.

Can I ask about my personal situation?

General questions are answered live. For personal or financial matters, book a private consultation.
