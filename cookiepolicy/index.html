DIGITAL TRACKING & COOKIES

Cookie Policy Guide

Learn how Sharif Group uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website to enhance your browsing experience and secure your digital interactions.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files downloaded and stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, recognize your device, and ensure secure navigation across various pages.

2. Scope of Cookie Usage

This Cookie Policy applies directly to the official Sharif Group website operated from Business Bay, Dubai. It explains how we utilize digital tracking tools to optimize our citizenship and residency advisory platform for global users.

3. Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function properly, enabling core features such as page navigation, form submissions, and secure user sessions. Without these cookies, our online consultation booking forms cannot operate smoothly.

4. Performance & Analytics Cookies

We utilize analytics cookies to understand how visitors interact with our web pages, track which residency programs receive the most traffic, and identify technical errors to continuously improve our digital presentation.

5. Functionality & Preference Cookies

Preference cookies allow our website to remember choices you make—such as your preferred language (English, Arabic, Russian, French, etc.) or regional timezone settings—to deliver a personalized advisory experience.

6. Targeting & Marketing Cookies

With your explicit consent, marketing cookies may be used to deliver relevant advertisements about second passports and Golden Visas across external networks, tracking campaign effectiveness without identifying you personally.

7. Third-Party Cookie Integration

Certain embedded features—such as Google Maps for our Business Bay office location, interactive video players, or social media sharing widgets—may set third-party cookies managed entirely by external providers.

8. Session vs. Persistent Cookies

We utilize temporary session cookies that expire automatically the moment you close your browser, alongside persistent cookies that remain on your device for a set duration to remember your device settings on future visits.

9. Managing Cookie Settings

You possess full control over your cookie preferences. Most standard web browsers allow you to block or delete existing cookies through your privacy or security settings menu at any time.

10. Impact of Disabling Cookies

Please be aware that if you choose to disable or reject essential cookies, certain interactive tools on our website—such as the eligibility checker or consultation scheduling forms—may not function properly.

11. Explicit Cookie Consent

When you first access our website, a notification banner requests your consent for non-essential cookie tracking. Continuing to navigate our platform indicates your ongoing agreement to our tracking protocols.

12. Data Anonymization

Analytical data gathered via cookies is processed in an aggregated and anonymized format whenever possible, ensuring that your individual identity remains completely protected while helping us evaluate site traffic.

13. Mobile & Cross-Device Tracking

Our cookies operate across both desktop and mobile viewports, ensuring seamless responsiveness, adaptive layouts, and consistent user preferences whether you browse via smartphone, tablet, or laptop.

14. Alignment with Privacy Policy

Any personal data collected automatically through cookie tracking is managed in strict accordance with our official Privacy Policy, ensuring high standards of confidentiality and secure information handling.

15. No Commercial Data Trading

Tracking identifiers and statistical logs collected via cookies are never sold, bartered, or shared with external third-party advertisers for independent commercial monetization or targeted telemarketing campaigns.

16. Periodic Policy Updates

We review and update our Cookie Policy regularly to align with new digital technologies, browser developments, or evolving regulatory requirements in the United Arab Emirates. Changes are published immediately on this page.

17. Secure Script Execution

All scripts and tracking pixels integrated into our web platform are vetted by our technical development team to prevent unauthorized data logging and maintain secure interactions for all visiting clients.

18. International Compliance

Our tracking practices respect global privacy expectations, ensuring that international high-net-worth investors from Europe, the Americas, and Asia experience transparent cookie disclosures while exploring our advisory portfolio.

19. Inquiries & Assistance

Should you have any questions or require technical assistance regarding our cookie usage or browser settings, you can reach out directly to our Business Bay support desk via email at contact@sharifgroup.ae.

20. Acceptance of Cookie Terms

By continuing to use the Sharif Group website without modifying your browser cookie settings, you signify your full agreement and acceptance of our cookie tracking guidelines and data collection practices.
