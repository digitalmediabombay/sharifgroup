ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Who Can Apply

Clear requirements for students applying to boarding schools, undergraduate degrees, and master's programs across the UK and Europe.

Student Applicants

Academic and entry requirements

  • Boarding school applicants aged 7 to 18 years.

  • High school graduates applying for Bachelor's degree programs.

  • University graduates seeking Master's and Postgraduate degrees.

  • Official school transcripts, marksheets, or degree certificates.

  • Basic English language proficiency (IELTS, TOEFL, or school assessments where required).

  • Letters of recommendation from teachers, school heads, or university professors.

  • A well-written personal statement and academic CV.

Applications Cannot Proceed If:

  • The student cannot provide official, certified academic records.

  • The applicant has previous visa refusals that were not resolved.

Parents & Sponsors

Financial and guardianship support

  • Parents or legal guardians acting as official financial sponsors.

  • Proof of funds covering annual school or university tuition fees.

  • Bank statements meeting the mandatory 28-day holding rule for UK visas.

  • Ability to open a blocked account (Sperrkonto) for Germany study routes.

  • Official parental consent letter for students under 18 years of age.

  • Appointed UK or Swiss local guardian for younger boarding students.

  • Parent visa options available for families accompanying young boarding students.
