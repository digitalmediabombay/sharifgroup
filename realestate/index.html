INVESTOR ELIGIBILITY

Who Can Invest & Qualify

Clear requirements for foreign buyers, local residents, and families looking to buy Dubai property or secure a UAE Golden Visa.

Property Buyers

Who can buy real estate in Dubai

  • Foreign nationals of all nationalities (no UAE residence visa required to purchase).

  • Buyers at least 21 years of age with a valid passport.

  • 100% full freehold property ownership in approved investment zones.

  • Individual investors, joint buyers (spouses or partners), and corporate entities.

  • Full access to both off-plan launches and completed ready properties.

  • Funds must be transferred legally via approved banking or escrow channels.

Purchase Restrictions:

  • Foreigners cannot buy freehold property in areas designated strictly for UAE/GCC nationals.

  • Cannot proceed without standard Anti-Money Laundering (AML) banking clearance.

UAE Golden Visa (10 Years)

Residency qualification through property

  • Minimum total property value of AED 2,000,000 (approx. $545,000 USD).

  • Can be one single luxury home or combined across multiple properties.

  • Both completed ready homes and approved off-plan projects qualify.

  • Mortgaged properties are allowed with a bank no-objection letter.

  • Sponsor your legal spouse, children of any age, and domestic staff.

  • No minimum stay required — visa stays active even if you remain outside the UAE.

Sharif Group works directly under Dubai Land Department (DLD) and RERA regulations to coordinate both property acquisitions and subsequent residency filings.

Required Documents

Simple paperwork checklist needed to buy property in Dubai and apply for your UAE Golden Visa.

1. For Individual Property Buyers

Valid Passport

Original & Clear Copy

Must have at least 6 months validity for title registration.

National ID / Emirates ID

If Applicable

Emirates ID for UAE residents or national identification card for overseas buyers.

Proof of Address

Recent Record

A utility bill or bank statement showing your residential address in your home country.

Sales Agreement (Form F / SPA)

Official Contract

Signed developer purchase contract (SPA) or RERA Form F unified agreement.

2. For UAE 10-Year Golden Visa (Property Route)

Title Deed (AED 2M+)

DLD Issued

Official title deed or Oqood certificate proving total property value of at least AED 2M.

Passport Photos

White Background

Recent passport-sized photographs matching official UAE immigration standards.

UAE Medical Test

Local Health Center

Standard local medical fitness check completed in Dubai after file registration.

UAE Health Insurance

Approved Coverage

Valid health insurance policy covering the applicant inside the United Arab Emirates.

3. For Family Sponsorship (Spouse & Children)

Attested Marriage Certificate

For Spouse

Original marriage certificate legalised and attested by UAE MOFA with official Arabic translation.

Attested Birth Certificates

For Children

Children's birth certificates attested by your home country's foreign ministry and the UAE Embassy.

Power of Attorney (Optional)

Remote Execution

Notarised POA if you prefer our Dubai legal desk to handle all filings on your behalf.
