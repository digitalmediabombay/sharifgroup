ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Who Can Apply

Clear requirements showing who can qualify as the main applicant and which family members can be included under the Greece Golden Visa program.

Principal Applicant Must:

Main investor qualifications

  • Be a non-EU/EEA citizen.

  • Be at least 18 years of age.

  • Have a clean criminal record in home country and globally.

  • Have valid health insurance covering medical expenses in Greece.

  • Complete a qualifying property investment (€250k, €400k, or €800k tier).

Applicants Are Not Eligible If They:

  • Are citizens of the European Union or European Economic Area.

  • Cannot prove the legal source of their investment funds.

  • Fail background checks or pose a security risk to public order in Greece.

Eligible Dependants:

Family members included in one application

  • A legal spouse or registered partner under a Greek cohabitation agreement.

  • Unmarried children under the age of 21.

  • Parents of the main applicant (no age limits or separate dependency proof required).

  • Parents of the spouse (parents-in-law are also fully covered).

All family members receive the exact same 5-year renewable residence permit, allowing full access to European travel, education, and healthcare benefits.
