Government Fees And Additional Costs

When applying for the Portugal Golden Visa through our Dubai office, applicants must account for statutory administrative expenses alongside their core investment. These include processing applications with AIMA (Agency for Integration, Migration and Asylum), biometric appointment fees, and mandatory legal representation costs.

Initial application processing fees and background check fees apply to each family member included in the file and vary according to the number of qualifying dependents. Furthermore, applicants must budget for bank transfer costs, official document translations, and Apostille certifications.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Who Can Apply

Clear and simple requirements showing who can apply as the main investor and which family members can be included.

Principal Applicant Must:

Main file owner qualifications

  • Be a non-EU, non-EEA, and non-Swiss national.

  • Be at least 18 years of age.

  • Hold a completely clean criminal record in both your home country and Portugal.

  • Demonstrate sufficient, lawful funds originating from outside of Portugal.

  • Commit to maintaining a qualifying investment for a minimum of 5 years.

  • Obtain a Portuguese Tax Number (NIF) and open a local bank account.

Applicants Are Not Eligible If They:

  • Are citizens or passport holders of the EU, EEA, or Switzerland.

  • Have a criminal history or open criminal investigations.

  • Have been banned or flagged as a security risk within the Schengen Area.

Eligible Dependants:

Family members you can include in one file

  • A spouse or legal partner.

  • Children under 18 years old.

  • Adult children over 18 if they are unmarried, financially dependent, and enrolled in full-time education.

  • Dependent parents of either the main applicant or the spouse (automatically approved if over 65, or requiring proof of dependency if under 65).

  • Dependent siblings under 18 years old, provided the main applicant holds legal guardianship.

Note: All dependants receive identical residence rights, meaning they can freely live, study, work, and travel throughout the Schengen Area.
