Securing your UAE 10-Year Golden Residency involves official governmental fees set by the Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security (ICP) and the Dubai Land Department (DLD). ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Who Can Apply

Clear and simple requirements showing who can apply as the main applicant and which family members can be included.

Principal Applicant Must:

Main file owner qualifications

  • Be at least 18 years of age.

  • Pass a mandatory medical fitness test conducted inside the UAE.

  • Meet the specific criteria for one of the main eligibility streams (Real Estate Investors, Exceptional Talents, Top Students, or Public Investors).

Applicants Are Not Eligible If They:

  • Fail the medical fitness test (testing positive for specific infectious or contagious health conditions).

  • Fail the strict federal security background checks.

  • Liquidate or sell the qualifying investment during the active residency period (investments must be maintained to keep the visa valid).

Eligible Dependants:

Family members you can include in one file

  • A legal spouse.

  • Children of any age (unlike traditional visas, there is no age cutoff for sponsoring dependent sons or daughters under the Golden Visa).

  • Parents (the main applicant can sponsor their mother and father for the full duration of the residency).

  • An unlimited number of domestic helpers (such as nannies, drivers, or housekeepers).

All dependants must pass basic health fitness screenings and standard UAE background security clearances. Applications are processed seamlessly through official channels and authorized typing or advisory desks in Dubai.
