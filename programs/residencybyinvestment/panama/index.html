Government Fees And Additional Costs

The headline investment amount is not the total cost. When calculating your budget for Panama residency, applicants must account for statutory government charges, treasury processing fees, and mandatory legal representation costs.

Under the Qualified Investor scheme, standard government fees include a $5,000 National Treasury fee and a $5,000 immigration application fee per main applicant, with nominal additional charges for included family dependents. Property purchases also involve standard public registry fees and legal notary expenses.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Who Can Apply

Comprehensive requirements showing who can apply as the principal investor and which family members can be included.

Main applicant

Main file owner qualifications

  • Non-Panamanian national, at least 18 years old.

  • Clean and properly legalised criminal record.

  • Qualifying investment funded from outside Panama.

  • Documented lawful source and traceability of funds.

  • Medical certificate meeting current validity rules.

  • No language, education or management-experience test for residence.

  • Separate work authorisation required for employment in Panama.

Applicants Are Not Eligible If They:

  • Cannot pass strict banking due diligence and anti-money laundering compliance.

  • Have serious criminal convictions or international security flags.

Eligible dependants

Family members you can include in one file

  • Legally married spouse.

  • Unmarried children under 18.

  • Unmarried, financially dependent full-time students aged 18–25.

  • Children with certified disabilities, without the standard age limit.

  • Financially dependent parents of the main applicant.

  • Relationship and dependency evidence must be apostilled or legalised.

  • Non-marital and same-sex relationships require case-specific legal review.

All applications for the Panama Qualified Investor Program are filed through authorized legal representatives. Biometrics appointments in Panama City are mandatory for all applicants over 18 years of age.
