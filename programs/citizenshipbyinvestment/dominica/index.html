ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Who Can Apply?

Clear and simple rules showing who qualifies for the primary application and family inclusion.

Principal Applicant Requirements

Main file owner qualifications

  • Be at least 18 years of age at the time of application submission.

  • Possess an outstanding clean criminal record with zero criminal history.

  • Satisfy all government program financial and basic health clearance criteria.

  • Provide complete proof showing clear and legal origins of all investment capital.

* Every applicant undergoes strict official background screening by government due diligence teams.

Eligible Family Members

Family members you can include in one file

  • Your legal spouse.

  • Dependent children under the age of 18.

  • Full-time student children aged 18 to 30 supported by the main applicant.

  • Unmarried daughters under 25 living with and fully supported by parents.

  • Children with physical or mental challenges regardless of age.

  • Dependent parents or grandparents over 65 years old.

* Applications must be processed via authorized agents like Sharif Group. Direct government submissions are not accepted.
DOCUMENTATION CHECKLIST

Required Documents

The essential paperwork needed to complete your application dossier smoothly.

Original Passport Copies

Clear notarized color copies of current valid passports for all family members.

Civil & Birth Certificates

Official certified birth records, national ID cards, and marriage certificates if applying with spouse.

Address Verification

Recent official utility bill or tenancy agreement showing your permanent home address.

Bank Reference Letter

Official letter issued by your primary bank confirming good account standing over 12 months.

Health & Medical Check

Standard medical report and HIV checkup results completed by an authorized medical doctor.

Wealth Statements & Proof

Documented statements confirming legal source of wealth, business ownership, or employment income.

Police Clearance Reports

Official clean police certificates from country of citizenship and any country resided in over 10 years.

Professional Reference & Letter

Recommendation letter from a licensed lawyer, accountant, or authorized corporate body.

The minimum investment for a single applicant starts at $200,000 USD via the Economic Diversification Fund (EDF) contribution, or $200,000 USD through pre-approved real estate (plus relevant government fees).

The complete processing timeline typically takes between 6 to 9 months from document submission to the official issuance of your Naturalisation Certificate and passport.

No, the entire process is 100% remote. There are zero physical residency or arrival requirements before, during, or after your citizenship is granted.

You can include your legal spouse, dependent children under 30 (studying full-time), unmarried daughters under 25, dependent parents/grandparents over 65, and financially dependent unmarried siblings under 25.

The EDF is a non-refundable sovereign government fund used to finance national public infrastructure, healthcare, education, and economic development initiatives in Dominica.

You invest a minimum of $200,000 USD in government-approved real estate, such as luxury resort shares or eco-villas, which must be held for a mandatory period of 3 to 5 years before reselling.

Yes, Dominica fully permits dual citizenship. The government does not require you to renounce your current nationality, and file confidentiality is strictly maintained.

Yes, a mandatory virtual or in-person interview is required for the main applicant and all dependants aged 16 and older as part of standard due diligence procedures ($1,000 USD per person).

A Dominica passport provides visa-free or visa-on-arrival entry to over 130 to 140 countries, including European Schengen nations, Hong Kong, Singapore, and key Latin American destinations.

Yes, Dominica citizenship is granted for life through an official Certificate of Naturalisation, which is legally protected and can be passed down to future generations.
