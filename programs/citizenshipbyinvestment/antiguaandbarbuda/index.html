INVESTMENT OPTIONS & COSTS

Explore Investment Options & Costs for Antigua & Barbuda

4 Approved Investment Routes

1. National Development Fund (NDF)

A direct donation starting from $230,000 USD for a single applicant or a family up to 4 members.

2. University of the West Indies (UWI) Fund

Available for families of 6 or more starting at $260,000 USD. Includes a 1-year university scholarship.

3. Approved Real Estate

Buy government-approved property with a minimum investment of $300,000 USD (must hold for 5 years).

4. Business Investment

Invest in an approved business starting from $400,000 USD for joint applicants or $1,500,000 USD for sole investors.

Government fees and additional costs

In addition to the purchase of real estate or your chosen contribution, there are official government processing fees, mandatory background due diligence charges, and legal administrative costs to be paid.

Family-oriented structures ensure that one primary fee can cover the main applicant, spouse, and qualifying dependents, with clear tiered rates for extra family members, parents, and siblings.

University of the West Indies (UWI) Fund

FAMILIES OF 6+ ONLY

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Who Can Apply

Clear and simple requirements showing who can apply as the main applicant and which family members can be included.

Principal Applicant Must:

Main file owner qualifications

  • Be at least 18 years of age.

  • Submit the application through a licensed agent.

  • Successfully complete the government's due diligence process.

  • Complete all government processing and passport formalities.

  • Take the Oath or Affirmation of Allegiance, either in Antigua and Barbuda or at an embassy, high commission, or consular office.

Eligible Dependants:

Family members you can include in one file

  • A spouse.

  • A child aged 0 to 30 who is financially dependent on the principal applicant.

  • A child aged 18 years or over who is physically or mentally handicapped and fully supported by the principal applicant.

  • A parent or grandparent aged 55 years or over who is financially dependent on the principal applicant.

  • An unmarried sibling of the principal applicant or spouse.

* The programme also allows certain future spouse and future child additions, subject to the applicable government fees.
