ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Who Can Apply

To qualify for the Nauru Economic and Climate Resilience Citizenship Program, applicants must satisfy the program's legal, financial, and due diligence requirements established by the Government of Nauru.

Principal Applicant Must:

Main file owner qualifications

  • Be at least 18 years of age.

  • Demonstrate good character and have no criminal record.

  • Demonstrate full financial capacity to make the required government treasury contribution.

  • Successfully pass the rigorous government due diligence review.

* Applicants must provide clean police clearance records from every country resided in for over six months over the past 10 years.

Due Diligence & Family:

Screening standards and inclusion rules

  • Applications can be denied if linked to criminal activities, terrorism, sanctions, or threats to public safety.

  • Principal applicants and qualifying adult dependents must complete scheduled background interviews.

  • Successful applicants must complete the Oath of Allegiance before final citizenship certificates are issued.

  • Broad family inclusion covers spouses, children, dependent parents, and siblings under program guidelines.

* Applications must be filed through authorized agents like Sharif Group for government review.
