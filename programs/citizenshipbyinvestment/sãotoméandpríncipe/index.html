Program Overview:
São Tomé & Príncipe

The São Tomé and Príncipe Citizenship by Investment Program is one of the newest government-administered options in the market. Established under national laws, it gives investors a direct way to obtain second citizenship through a contribution to the National Transformation Fund (NTF). Managed out of our Dubai Business Bay office, it offers low entry costs, fast processing, and no need to move or live on the island.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Who Can Apply

Clear and simple requirements showing who can apply as the main applicant and which family members can be included.

Principal Applicant Must:

Main file owner qualifications

  • Be at least 18 years of age.

  • Have no criminal record.

  • Demonstrate a legitimate source of income and investment funds.

  • Successfully pass the programme's comprehensive due diligence process.

  • Submit a complete application with all required supporting documentation.

The programme places particular emphasis on source-of-funds verification, document completeness, and compliance screening throughout the application process.

Eligible Dependants:

Family members you can include in one file

  • A spouse in a legally recognised monogamous marriage or de facto partnership.

  • Children up to 30 years of age, subject to the programme's eligibility requirements.

  • Parents or grandparents aged 55 or over.

The programme also permits the addition of certain qualifying dependants after approval under the published government fee schedule. Applications must be submitted through licensed marketing agents authorised by the programme. Official programme guidance indicates that applications are generally processed within approximately two to three months.

The essential paperwork needed to complete your application dossier smoothly.

These basic forms are generated by your processing agency and must be fully completed.

Citizenship Application Form

Standard Personal Detail Sheet

Financial Sponsorship

2. Core Identity Documents (For Individual & Family)

Every person included in the application must provide high-quality, clear records of who they are.

Passports

Valid Color Copies

High-resolution color copies of all pages of your current national passport and any foreign passports.

National Identification

Government ID or License

Certified copies of a national ID card or a valid driving license.

Birth Certificate

Full Certified Extract

Certified copy of a long-format birth certificate showing parental information.

Passport Photos

Recent Photographs

Recent, high-resolution passport-style photographs against a white background.

3. Financial Proofs & Source of Wealth (Main Applicant Only)

Unlike Caribbean programs, São Tomé and Príncipe has a very lenient financial policy. It does not strictly require intensive tax returns or multi-year corporate audits to justify employment status. However, you must prove the legitimacy of your current funds:

Proof of Legitimate Income

Employer Letter / Docs

A basic letter from an employer or simple documentation verifying your income source.

Most Recent Tax Return

Latest Filing (If Available)

A copy of your latest tax filing if available.

Bank Reference Letter

Active Healthy Account

An original letter from a recognized international bank confirming an active, healthy account.

Proof of Address

Utility Bill or Lease Agreement

A recent utility bill or a legal lease agreement verifying your current residential address.

4. Background & Health Documents (Adults Only)

Police Clearance Certificate

Criminal Background Check

An original criminal background check from your country of citizenship and any country where you have lived for the past 5 years. Note: The program uniquely allows you to choose to provide this from either your country of residence or nationality to speed up the process.

Medical Certificate

Good Health Assessment

A signed assessment from a licensed doctor confirming that the applicants are in general good health and free from highly contagious conditions.

5. Extra Relationship Records for Family (Dependents)

To claim the special family package—which allows up to four family members for a flat donation fee—you must legally verify the family unit:

For a Spouse

Marriage Certificate

An official, certified copy of your legal Marriage Certificate.

For Dependent Children (Under 30)

Affidavit & Enrollment

An Affidavit of Financial Support signed by the main investor, alongside school or university enrollment letters if the child is an adult student.

For Parents & Grandparents (Age 55+)

Lineage Records

Multi-generational birth certificates establishing the family lineage back to the primary investor, paired with a formal statement of financial dependence.
