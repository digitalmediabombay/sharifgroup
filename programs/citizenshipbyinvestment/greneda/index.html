Program Overview:
Grenada

Managed under the Grenada Citizenship by Investment Act, the program offers a clear and legally protected path to second citizenship. Supervised by the Investment Migration Agency Grenada (IMA Grenada), it lets investors and their families secure dual citizenship through a donation or real estate. Handled directly by our Dubai office, it gives you simple processing and long-term peace of mind.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Who Can Apply

Clear and simple requirements showing who can apply as the main applicant and which family members can be included.

Principal Applicant Must:

Main file owner qualifications

  • Be at least 18 years of age.

  • Be in good health.

  • Have no criminal record.

  • Demonstrate sufficient lawful funds to complete the qualifying investment.

Applicants Are Not Eligible If They:

  • Are under criminal investigation.

  • Are considered a security risk.

  • Have engaged in conduct likely to bring disrepute to Grenada.

  • Have previously been denied a visa to a country with which Grenada has visa-free travel, unless that visa was subsequently granted.

Eligible Dependants:

Family members you can include in one file

  • A spouse.

  • Children.

  • Parents.

  • Grandparents.

  • Siblings.

All dependants must satisfy the programme's published dependency requirements. The programme does not specify a fixed limit on the number of qualifying family members, provided each dependant meets the official eligibility criteria. Applications must be submitted through an Authorised International Marketing Agent working together with an Authorised Local Agent. An online interview forms part of the due diligence process.

You must purchase a fractional share or full title in a government-approved resort or hotel project starting at $270,000 USD. You are required to maintain ownership for at least 5 years, after which you can sell the asset to another investor while keeping your citizenship permanently.
