ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Who Can Apply?

Clear and simple rules showing who qualifies for the primary application and family inclusion.

Principal Applicant Requirements

Main file owner qualifications

  • Be at least 18 years of age at the time of application submission.

  • Possess an outstanding clean criminal record with zero criminal history.

  • Satisfy all government program financial and basic health clearance criteria.

  • Provide complete proof showing clear and legal origins of all investment capital.

* Every applicant undergoes strict official background screening by government due diligence teams.

Eligible Family Members

Family members you can include in one file

  • Your legal spouse.

  • Dependent children under the age of 18.

  • Full-time student children aged 18 to 30 supported by the main applicant.

  • Unmarried daughters under 25 living with and fully supported by parents.

  • Children with physical or mental challenges regardless of age.

  • Dependent parents or grandparents over 65 years old.

* Applications must be processed via authorized agents like Sharif Group. Direct government submissions are not accepted.

Everything you need to know about getting St. Kitts & Nevis citizenship

The minimum investment starts at $250,000 USD through the Sustainable Island State Contribution (SISC) or Public Benefit Option. If you prefer real estate, the minimum starts at $400,000 USD in a government-approved development project.

The standard process usually takes between 4 to 6 months from the day your application is submitted to the day you receive your official citizenship certificate and passport.

No, you do not need to visit or reside in St. Kitts & Nevis at any point. The entire application process can be handled completely remotely from our Business Bay office in Dubai.

You can include your legal spouse, dependent children under 18, unmarried student children aged 18 to 25 enrolled full-time in higher education, and dependent parents or grandparents aged 65 and older.

SISC is an official non-refundable government contribution route. Money paid into this fund directly supports green energy, healthcare, local infrastructure, and sustainable growth across the islands.

When you buy approved developer real estate ($400,000+) or private property ($800,000+), you must hold the property for a mandatory period of at least 7 years before you are legally permitted to resell it.

Yes, St. Kitts & Nevis fully recognizes and allows dual citizenship. You do not need to give up your current nationality, and your application details remain completely confidential.

Yes, a mandatory online video interview is conducted by government officials for the main applicant. Dependants aged 16 and older may also be asked to join a short interview session.

The government due diligence fee is $10,000 USD for the main applicant and $7,500 USD for each dependant aged 16 and older. Dependants under 16 do not pay due diligence fees.

A St. Kitts & Nevis passport gives you visa-free or visa-on-arrival entry to over 145 to 155 countries worldwide, including the UK, European Schengen countries, Singapore, and Hong Kong.

Yes, citizenship is permanent and granted for life. Once issued, your Certificate of Naturalisation cannot be canceled, and citizenship can be passed on to future children.

No, there are no language tests, history exams, or specific educational requirements needed to qualify for the St. Kitts & Nevis citizenship program.

No, the St. Kitts & Nevis Citizenship by Investment Unit only accepts files submitted through licensed, authorized agents like Sharif Group.

St. Kitts & Nevis offers a highly attractive tax climate with zero personal income tax, zero gift tax, zero inheritance tax, and no tax on worldwide income or capital gains for non-residents.

You will need valid passport copies, birth certificates, police clearance reports, 12 months of bank statements, proof of home address, a basic medical report, and proof of legal income.

You only transfer your main investment amount (the $250,000 SISC donation or real estate balance) AFTER your application has been officially reviewed and approved by the government.

St. Kitts & Nevis passport holders can visit the United Kingdom for business or tourism for up to 6 months per visit under an Electronic Travel Authorization (eTA).

Passports can be easily renewed every 10 years through any St. Kitts & Nevis consulate worldwide or directly through our Sharif Group office in Dubai without traveling.

Yes, any child born after you receive citizenship can be registered for citizenship by descent through a simple administrative submission process.
