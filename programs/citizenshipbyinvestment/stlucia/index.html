Program Overview:
Saint Lucia

The Saint Lucia Citizenship by Investment Programme is one of the most flexible in the Caribbean. Administered by the official Citizenship by Investment Unit (CIU), it offers four qualifying pathways: a donation to the National Economic Fund (NEF), National Action Government Bonds (NAB), approved real estate, or enterprise projects. It gives you strong legal safety, clear requirements, and flexible options for your whole family.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Who Can Apply

Clear requirements showing who can apply as the principal investor and which family dependants can be included.

Principal Applicant Must:

Main file owner qualifications

  • Be at least 18 years of age.

  • Complete one of the programme's four qualifying investment routes.

  • Provide documentary evidence of the proposed investment.

  • Successfully pass the government's due diligence background checks.

  • Make full and frank financial disclosure throughout the application process.

Applications must be submitted through a licensed Authorised Agent like Sharif Group. Direct applications are not accepted.

Eligible Dependants:

Family members you can include in one file

  • A legal spouse.

  • A child of the applicant or spouse who is 21 years of age or younger.

  • A child up to 30 years of age who is fully supported by the principal applicant and enrolled in higher education.

  • A child of any age who is physically or mentally challenged and fully supported by the principal applicant.

  • A parent of the applicant or spouse aged over 55 who is fully supported by the principal applicant.

  • An unmarried sister or brother under the age of 18 with parental consent.

Due diligence applies to all qualifying dependants over the age of 16, along with mandatory interview and oath-taking protocols.

No, Saint Lucia does not require any physical residency before, during, or after your citizenship application. You are not required to visit or reside in Saint Lucia to maintain your status, allowing you to complete the entire process remotely from our Dubai advisory office.

Saint Lucia offers four distinct routes: a non-refundable contribution to the National Economic Fund (NEF) from $240,000, National Action Government Bonds (NAB) from $300,000 held for 5 years, government-approved luxury Real Estate from $300,000, or approved Enterprise Projects starting from $3,500,000 for sole investors.

You can include your legal spouse, dependent children up to 21 years old (or up to 30 if enrolled full-time in higher education), physically/mentally challenged children of any age, financially dependent parents over 55, and unmarried siblings under the age of 18 with parental consent.

The NEF is an official government financial fund established under Section 33 of the Citizenship by Investment Act. It finances sovereign infrastructure projects, healthcare facilities, education systems, and local economic growth initiatives across Saint Lucia.

Under the NAB route, applicants purchase non-interest-bearing government bonds starting at $300,000 USD, accompanied by a $50,000 USD non-refundable administration fee. The bonds are held in the investor’s name for a mandatory period of 5 years, after which the initial capital is fully returned by the government.

Yes, you can qualify by acquiring government-approved real estate—typically luxury beachfront resorts, villas, or high-end hotel developments—with a minimum investment of $300,000 USD. Property assets must be retained for at least 5 years before they can be resold.

Yes, Saint Lucia fully recognizes dual citizenship under sovereign law. You are not required to give up your current nationality, and the government maintains strict confidentiality regarding all citizenship applications without notifying your home country.

No, there are no mandatory language proficiency tests, interviews on local history or culture, educational degree requirements, or business management experience required to qualify for citizenship by investment.
